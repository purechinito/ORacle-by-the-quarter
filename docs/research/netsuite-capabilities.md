# NetSuite capability and replacement research

Researched **2026-09-28**, using public Oracle/NetSuite primary documentation. This note establishes documented product behavior; it does **not** confirm the target account's licensed modules, enabled features, customizations, user permissions, data completeness, or AI eligibility. No account access or account changes were performed for this research.

## What “functionally faithful” must cover

NetSuite's documented ERP scope includes accounting, inventory, customer orders, purchasing, projects, and employee management. Optional areas include revenue recognition, fixed assets, multi-book accounting, manufacturing, and payroll. Treat these as discovery candidates, not as confirmed requirements for this account. [ERP documentation summary](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N129046.html)

The table separates documented behavior from proposed evidence needed to prove parity. The final column is an engineering recommendation, not an Oracle guarantee.

| Area | Documented behavior | Account evidence and parity checks needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quote to order | NetSuite uses estimate and quote interchangeably. An accepted estimate can become a sales order, invoice, or cash sale. [Estimate conversion](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1073352.html) | Capture pricing, discounts, tax, mandatory custom fields, approval thresholds, terms, and source-to-result transaction links. |
| Fulfillment to billing | Advanced Shipping separates fulfillment from billing; otherwise they occur together. Pick/pack/ship, partial fulfillment, and optional fulfillment requests change the flow. [Order fulfillment](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_4737787881.html) | Verify feature flags, committed/backordered quantities, partial shipments, fulfillment status, inventory impact, invoice quantities, and closed lines. |
| Receivables and collections | Customer payments can apply to invoices with credits and deposits. Consolidated Payments changes whether parent and subcustomer balances can be paid together. [Customer payments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1285644.html) | Preserve payment applications, remaining balances, unapplied cash, deposits, currencies, aging, refunds, and bank reconciliation behavior. |
| Purchase to receipt | Purchase orders track expected and received items and do not themselves post accounting impact. Advanced Receiving separates receipt from vendor billing; partial receipts are supported. [Purchase orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2399585.html), [receiving](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2410585.html) | Capture requisitions/approvals if used, line-level outstanding quantities, receipt links, landed costs, returns, and over-receipt preferences. |
| Bills and vendor payments | Vendor bills can be created from POs. Receipt matching supports quantity/rate variance handling. Bills can be paid in groups with credits; subsidiary and currency affect eligibility. [PO billing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2403862.html), [variance handling](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2399585.html), [paying vendors](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2383803.html) | Prove AP balances, partial payments, applications, credits, payment approvals, bank files/provider integrations, and duplicate-bill behavior. Recording a payment and moving funds require separate verification. |
| Inventory | Quantities, costs, and asset values interact across purchasing, receiving, manufacturing, selling, fulfillment, and replenishment. Transfer orders track movement and inventory in transit. [Inventory workflow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2251098.html), [transfer orders](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2308766.html) | Inventory is a transaction ledger, not just an editable stock count. Reconcile quantities and value by item/location plus every enabled bin, lot, serial, and status dimension. |
| Inventory detail and valuation | Basic and advanced bin features differ; bins do not maintain separate costing. Costing methods and inventory adjustments affect valuation history. [Bins](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2270284.html), [costing methods](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N2191818.html), [adjustments](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_161981111273.html) | Identify actual costing methods, units of measure, count processes, negative inventory policy, reorder logic, and manufacturing/WMS usage. Test backdated changes and recalculation. |
| Financials and close | Financial statements include income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow. GL and trial balance reports are separate. Accounting periods control posting, locking, reopening, and close tasks. [Financial statements](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2092547.html), [financial reports](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N1520496.html), [period management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N1445226.html) | Reconcile debits/credits, chart of accounts, segments, fiscal periods, tax, currency rounding, realized/unrealized FX, journals, and period restrictions. Match report filters and accounting basis as well as totals. |
| Multi-entity accounting | OneWorld supports subsidiary hierarchies, currencies, intercompany transactions, and consolidation. Multi-Book is a separate, OneWorld-dependent capability. [OneWorld](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N266701.html), [Multi-Book](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_3831567542.html) | Discover whether either is used; preserve legal-entity boundaries, consolidation rates, eliminations, and book-specific results when applicable. |
| CRM | Oracle describes sales automation, marketing automation, customer support/service, sales performance, orders, and partner management. This is product-scope evidence, not proof that all modules are enabled. [NetSuite CRM data sheet](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/assets/public-pdf/ds-netsuite-crm-plus.pdf) | Inventory lead/prospect/customer stages, opportunities, activities, contacts, cases, campaigns, commissions, and external CRM integrations actually used. |
| Reporting and analytics | Workbook supports datasets, joins, formulas, tables, pivots, and charts. Its analytics source may require different fields and joins from existing saved searches. [Workbook overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1503949328.html), [data-source differences](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158874665729.html) | Inventory saved searches, custom reports, datasets, workbook formulas, dashboards, schedules, audiences, and exports; compare output on identical periods and permissions. |
| Workflows and extensions | SuiteFlow has record-specific states, actions, conditions, transitions, event triggers, and scheduled execution. Custom fields, forms, record types, scripts, and templates can alter behavior. [SuiteFlow](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_4068260113.html), [customization](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_N2824008.html) | Preserve business rules, approval routing, record locks, scheduled jobs, script side effects, attachments, printed documents, notifications, and integration contracts. |

## All users and roles means an authorization model

Roles govern record/task/page access and connect to role-specific centers. Users can include employees, vendors, partners, and customers. Permissions have levels and categories; record restrictions further narrow which individual records users can access. Global employee permissions, where enabled, can override role-based permissions. [Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N285436.html), [permissions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N325094.html), [restrictions](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_3781107123.html)

Recommended evidence: export a user-to-role matrix and each role's permissions/restrictions; include inactive users, service identities, role switching, subsidiary restrictions, self-service access, custom-record access, and authentication arrangements. A role selector and hidden navigation links do not prove authorization parity; test permitted and forbidden operations at the server boundary.

System Notes capture actor, time, interface/context, changed field, and old/new values, and cannot be edited by users, scripts, or apps. System Notes v2 additionally groups detailed changes and retains information for deleted records; coverage and access differ between the two systems. [System Notes](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_158644279544.html), [System Notes v2](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_158108495822.html)

Recommended parity: preserve accessible historical audit evidence with provenance; create an append-only audit trail for new actions, including AI/API actions, approvals, failed authorization attempts, and source-to-result transaction links. Do not imply migrated records automatically carry native NetSuite System Notes.

A prompt library is not a replacement accounting engine. |

**Unresolved:** the underlying model configuration for this account's Ask Oracle, its NetSuite Next eligibility, AI-unit entitlement, installed MCP SuiteApps, approved AI clients, and desired action scope. No researched source establishes that “Oracle Ask integrated with ChatGPT” is one native feature. Treat it as a likely reference to one or both paths above until the account confirms it.

Recommended replacement AI design: connect the assistant to the same permission-checked business services as the UI; cite records/reports used; present material proposed changes before execution; log tool arguments and outcomes; use idempotent transactions and deterministic accounting calculations. These are proposed implementation requirements, not claims about existing NetSuite behavior.

## Migration and performance constraints

1. **The account is the specification.** Records Catalog reflects enabled features and account customizations. Combine it with feature settings, roles, forms, scripts, workflows, SuiteApps, integrations, and representative transactions. [Records Catalog](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/article_159367781370.html)
2. **“Full CSV Export” is incomplete.** Oracle explicitly says it does not export all data. Saved searches/reports and other supported extraction channels may be needed. Preserve internal/external IDs, relationships, line identifiers, files, and audit evidence; maintain an extraction manifest and reconciliation results. [CSV export limits](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N464443.html), [extraction options](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/section_N464759.html)
3. **Metadata export is not engine portability.** SDF represents supported customization objects as XML, including roles, forms, fields, workflows, and scripts. That supplies useful specifications; it does not establish that NetSuite logic can execute unchanged outside NetSuite. Check individual object coverage and vendor-controlled SuiteApps. [SDF XML reference](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/SDFxml.html)
4. **API coverage has gaps.** REST does not expose custom sublists; legacy tax features are unsupported through REST and taxation requires SuiteTax. Determine each required operation's supported channel before committing to a live API-backed UI or migration plan. [REST limitations](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1544786455.html), [supported records](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_1558962745.html)
5. **A NetSuite-backed interface inherits upstream limits.** REST, other web services, and RESTlets share account concurrency governance. Asynchronous REST record processing supports idempotent retry, but asynchronous REST query execution is not supported. [Concurrency](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1559222360.html), [async processing](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/chapter_161251386312.html), [query limits](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/netsuite/ns-online-help/subsect_1544786455.html)

Recommended acceptance evidence before replacing production: reconciled trial balance and subledgers; AR/AP open-item applications; inventory quantities and valuation; representative end-to-end workflows including partial/reversed/error cases; per-role access tests; concurrent-write and retry tests; peak-volume latency measurements; backup/restore verification; and a cutover/delta/rollback procedure. Choose measurable performance targets after observing actual volumes and workflows. None of these has been validated by this public-documentation research.

## Remaining discovery questions

- Is the intended end state an independent ERP, or a new interface that keeps NetSuite as the system of record?
- Which enabled modules, subsidiaries, currencies, tax engines, localizations, and accounting books are actually used?
- Which users/roles, custom records/forms/scripts, workflows, SuiteApps, reports, scheduled jobs, and external integrations must be preserved?
- What historical depth, files, audit records, and open transactions must move, and what reconciliations define acceptance?
- What are actual record counts, transaction-line volumes, concurrent users, slow workflows, and operational recovery requirements?
- Which AI capabilities are already available in the account, and which actions should the replacement assistant be allowed to perform?
