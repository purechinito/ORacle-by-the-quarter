# Philippine demo

Use **Orbit Philippines Demo** for practice. It is synthetic and does not represent any of the five registered companies.

- Customer: **Orbit Philippines Demo Customer**
- Finished item: **ORBIT-PH-DEMO-BEARING**, PHP selling price 1,000 per unit
- Supplier: **Orbit Philippines Demo Supplier**
- Component: **ORBIT-PH-DEMO-PURCHASE-PART**, PHP buying price 500 per unit
- Warehouse: **Stores - OPH**
- Price lists: **PH Standard Selling** and **PH Standard Buying**
- Domestic taxable sales template: **PH VAT 12% Sales - OPH**, tax exclusive
- Purchase template: **PH VAT 12% Purchases - OPH**, using a separate input VAT asset account

A practice sale of two bearing kits at PHP 1,000 each gives PHP 2,000 net, PHP 240 output VAT and PHP 2,240 total. This example assumes a standard taxable domestic transaction. It does not determine the correct treatment of an actual transaction or establish eligibility for input VAT credits. Zero-rated/exempt transactions and withholding need their own accountant-approved treatment. [BIR VAT guidance](https://efps.bir.gov.ph/efps-war/EFPSWeb_war/help/help2550m2006.html).

New invoices load the Philippine company, PHP amounts and its VAT template. Reload already-open ERP tabs to load changed defaults. Existing drafts keep their original company and currency until reviewed; they are not converted automatically. The original US demo ledger is preserved as history and is not a peso ledger.

The Philippine setup does not issue a BIR-approved invoice series or create taxpayer registration evidence. Complete the five real company profiles and bookkeeper review before entering live business transactions.
