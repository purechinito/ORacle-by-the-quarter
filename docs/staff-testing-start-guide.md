## First owner-led test

1. Open [NGOSIOK MARKETING Sales](https://erp.superq.ph/orbit?section=sales&company=NGOSIOK%20MARKETING).
   Sign in with your existing authorized account. Check **NGOSIOK MARKETING · PHP**
   in the company selector.
2. Click **All ERP modules** and open the Customer and Item records through the
   ERP search. Complete one genuine customer's details. On the Item list, click
   **Clear all filters** if it looks empty: the default filter hides disabled
   imported items. Select one real imported item by code; confirm its stock unit
   and packaging conversion, relevant real
   warehouse and approved PHP selling price before enabling that item. Do not
   bulk-enable items with an unconfirmed `Unit` placeholder.
3. Return to Sales and click **New sales order**. Before entering data, choose
   which form to use. For delivery addresses, pricing rules and tax inputs, click
   **Open the full sales-order form**. It opens a separate blank form and does not
   transfer unsaved entries. Use only one form for this order.
4. Enter the real Customer, Company, delivery date, Item code, Warehouse, Quantity
   and verified PHP price. In the full form, confirm the actual tax treatment,
   including whether rates include VAT; do not infer it from a price. The simple
   form does not expose these tax/address controls and requires a warehouse. If
   required data is unknown, complete the masters/evidence before this first test.
5. Click **Save draft** in the simple form, or **Save** in the full ERP form, and
   inspect the calculated amounts and totals. Keep this first test as a draft;
   do not click Submit. Saving a draft does not move stock or post accounting.
6. If the simple form's save times out, use **Retry same save**. It preserves the
   original command identity. If a native form's outcome is unclear, check for
   the existing saved record before creating another. Do not enter the same order
   in both forms to work around an uncertain save.

Real customer balances must be imported/reconciled before the credit panel is
used for decisions. Zero booked balance on the current site does not establish
that a customer has paid all debts.

## Planned on-screen help

- **Start here:** the first useful field/button in a short guide.
- **Red dot + Action needed + count:** a genuine task this person can act on now.
- **Waiting for Winnie / plant / Finance:** another person owns the next step.
- **Completed:** the server has confirmed the action succeeded.

Each account will have a **Your next step** card and a Help me control. The guide
will use clear text as well as color, and will not grant access or approve a
transaction on a person's behalf. These indicators are a proposed change, not
existing behavior on the live site.
